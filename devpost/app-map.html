Follow one action

  1. The idea entry and recovery: find placeCurrent, persist and restore. The sentence is retained on cancellation; stale runs require explicit re-placement.
  2. The private worker and measurement core: public assets load from the same origin, hashes are verified, and the same WASM/vector contract is used locally and in public preparation.
  3. The interview and hand-off: startInterview fixes a majority-default queue capped at five. Answer, skip and stop control completion; a percentile never does.

Map appearance and interaction

Map.svelte draws hollow forecast rings, the personal point and its same-run trail. tokens.css contains the current visual theme and responsive rules.

Public matching rules and evidence

default-codebook-v1.json defines explicit wording patterns. analysis.py saves per-item assignments, cluster counts and provenance. These labels are exploratory and not human-validated semantic judgments.

Terminal companion

companion.ts reads the local profile and runs the interview interactively. companion-io.ts protects output paths and refuses overwrite. The companion never edits planning or Skill Pack files.

A practice to reuse

The frozen sampling run attempted 384 ideas and retained its one invalid result. Offline replay reproduced the artifact with zero model calls. That establishes repeatability and failure accounting; it does not establish forecasting accuracy. The frozen evaluation protocol separately requires a control and human checks of judge verdicts. Keep those claims separate on the next project, too.
